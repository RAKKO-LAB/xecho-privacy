Privacy Policy (Xecho)

Established: October 26, 2026
Revised: November 20, 2026

RAKKO-LAB ("we") sets out below how the Android app "Xecho" (the "App") handles users' information.

1. What the App does

The App uses Android's Accessibility feature (Accessibility API) to read the posts that the user displays on screen in the official X (formerly Twitter) app for Android, and records them only on the user's device. The user can read the recorded posts back in the App.

The App is an unofficial app and is not affiliated with X Corp.

2. Information the App reads

The App reads only what is displayed on screen in the official X app (package name com.twitter.android), and only while the user has turned the App on in Accessibility settings. It does not read the screens of any other app.

Items the App reads and saves:

  • The display name, username (the name starting with @) and text of each post, and the display name and text of any quoted post
  • The post time estimated from the time shown with the post (such as "3h")
  • The numbers shown on screen for replies, reposts, likes and views
  • The number of images and whether there is a video (images and videos themselves are not saved)
  • Whether the post is an ad, and the names shown as the person who reposted it or the account it replies to
  • The dates, times and number of times the user displayed each post
  • Logs for checking that the App works (whether reading succeeded, the type of screen, and the date and time; they contain no post text or names and are kept for 7 days only)

On a post's detail screen (the screen that opens when you open a post), X does not pass the text of that post to the Accessibility feature. So, only while X's detail screen is displayed, the App takes a screenshot, cuts out the part showing that post and reads the text on the device (text recognition uses Google's ML Kit with a model bundled in the App). The screenshot is not saved; it is discarded right after the text is read. On Android 14 and later, only X's window is captured. On Android 13 and earlier, the whole screen is captured, so if a notification or other display overlaps the post at that moment, its text may be read along with the post and saved as part of the items above (even then, it never leaves the device). The text that is read is saved as the items above. This screenshot can be turned off in the App's settings.

The App never operates X (no tapping, scrolling, liking, posting or anything else).

3. Where information is stored, and sending it outside the device

  • Everything is stored on the user's device.
  • The App does not have internet permission. Records are therefore never sent to our servers or to any third party's servers.
  • We cannot see the user's records.
  • The text recognition component (Google's ML Kit) and the in-app purchase component include mechanisms that send usage information, but because the App does not have internet permission, nothing is sent.
  • The App is also excluded from the device's backup features (such as Google backup).

4. Exports by the user

The App's settings screen has a feature to export records as a JSON file (in some versions only) and a feature to export the logs for checking that the App works (which contain no post text or names). Exports happen only when the user starts them, and the user chooses where to save the file in Android's file picker. The user is responsible for the exported files.

5. Automatic deletion and deletion

  • In the settings screen, the user can choose how long to keep records before they are deleted automatically (30 days / 90 days / never; the default is never). Posts the user has "saved" are never deleted automatically.
  • Posts can be deleted one at a time, or all at once from the settings screen.
  • Uninstalling the App deletes all records on the device.

6. Free trial, in-app purchase and yearly subscription

  • All features can be used free of charge for 3 days (72 hours) from the first record. Recording continues after that, but reading the records back requires an in-app purchase. There are two kinds: a one-time purchase and a yearly subscription. The yearly subscription renews automatically every year and can be cancelled at any time from Subscriptions in Google Play.
  • The purchase process (entering a payment method and payment) is handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive payment information such as credit card numbers. Google handles information under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  • We receive from Google Play the order information we need to manage sales (such as the order number, purchase date and time, amount and country).
  • The App receives whether the user has purchased it and whether a yearly subscription is active from the Google Play Store app on the device, and stores the result only on the device. So that records from a paid period stay readable after a subscription ends, the App marks, only on the device, the posts recorded while the subscription was active. To determine the trial period, it also stores, only on the device, the date and time the trial started and the latest date and time the App has seen. None of this is sent outside the device.
  • Purchases and subscriptions are tied to the Google account, so it comes back after reinstalling with the same account (the records themselves do not come back; see section 5).

7. Permissions used

  • Accessibility service (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE): for the reading described in section 2. It works only when the user turns it on in Accessibility settings, and can be turned off at any time.
  • Screenshots (canTakeScreenshot of the accessibility service): for reading the detail screen described in section 2. Screenshots are not saved. This is declared as a capability of the accessibility service, not as an Android permission.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to let the user know when recording has stopped.
  • In-app purchases (com.android.vending.BILLING): for in-app purchases through Google Play.

8. Sharing with third parties

The only information we obtain is the order information in section 6 and what you send through the contact form (section 12). We do not share it with any third party (except where required by law).

9. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18.

10. Disclaimer

The App is an unofficial app and has no connection with X Corp. or its affiliates. Changes to X may stop the App from recording. In that case we will work to fix it as soon as we can, but we may not always be able to, and purchases and subscription fees are not refunded in that case (except for refunds under Google Play's refund policy or where required by law). We are not liable for any damage arising from the use of the App.

11. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy. The changed policy takes effect when it is posted on this page.

12. Contact

The form opens in your browser with this app's version and display language, your device model, and the Android and X app versions already filled in (you can delete them before sending; your log is not included). What you send through the form is received by us via Google's service and used only to respond to your inquiry. Google handles this information under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
